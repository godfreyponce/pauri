## Gate 1 decisions (owner: change any of these before go)

1. **The pick happens at a stop after Task 1, not at gate 1.** As in #5, #8, #16, #17, #21, #34: after go, the build session builds the page and stops; Task 2 waits for your pick.
2. **References** (fixed by the ticket, for feel, not layout): the ChatGPT Mac window (`docs/reference/chatgpt-window.png`, shown on the page from its relative path) and Raycast AI chat. No Raycast image is in the repo; if you want it on the page, send a screenshot in your reply and Task 1 saves it as `docs/reference/raycast-ai-chat.png`. Otherwise the lede names Raycast only.
3. **"Keep the transcript layout" means no bubble and no right-aligned message.** Every variant keeps the user's message left-aligned on its row, the 44 pt label column, the answer indented under it, and the divider between exchanges. ChatGPT's right-aligned bubble is a reference for its contrast, not its shape. Alternative: allow a fifth reading where the user's message becomes a right-aligned bubble, which is #16's old shape that #21 replaced.
4. **The four shapes** (what marks the user's message):
   - **A. Tinted band.** The whole question row, label included, sits on a full-width rounded fill (a faint purple tint, about the strength of a selected list row). The answer below has no fill. Shape: a container.
   - **B. Accent bar.** A 2 pt purple vertical bar in the label column runs the full height of the question, every wrapped line; `YOU` moves above the bar or goes away (the page shows both, the note says which it proposes). The text has no fill. Shape: a margin mark.
   - **C. Accent ink.** No container and no bar: the question text itself turns purple (the link tint) at regular weight, and `YOU` becomes a small filled purple pill. Shape: the text's own color.
   - **D. Two speakers.** Both sides get a marker in the label column: a small person circle beside the question, and a small orb glyph (the send orb's look, static) beside the first line of the answer. The question has a hairline under it. Shape: who is talking, on both sides.
5. **One purple, from the app.** Color cues use the link tint: `0x8F86FF` in dark, `0x4A3DB8` in light, at lower opacity for a fill. Alternative: the system accent color (`Color.accentColor`), which follows the user's macOS setting and may clash with the purple links.
6. **The gap between question and answer is set per variant on the page**, and the winner's value carries into SwiftUI. Today it is 4 pt. The page starts each variant at 10 pt (a guess, to be judged by eye); the owner can ask for a different value at the pick.
7. **No new test.** The change is look only: no logic, no new state. The hand check in Task 2 is the proof. A snapshot test would pin pixels, not behavior.
