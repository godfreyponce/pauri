Pauri #5: tool step lines and approval cards

Four shapes, one set of data. Each panel shows a read-only question that already finished (three steps, one opened), then a write that waits on a card. Click a step line to open or close it. The card buttons work: Allow and Allow for this chat finish the step, Deny marks it (denied).
