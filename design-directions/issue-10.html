Pauri #10: the send orb, four Ghibli-feeling looks and four send animations

Each column is one look, painted in the mood of the owner's three Studio Ghibli references: a summer sky, a flower meadow, a seaside town at sunset, and a forest at dusk. Original motifs only, no characters. Pick the send animation at the top: it applies to every column. Each column shows the orb in a dark and a light input row at 1x (32 pt), then at 3x. Click Send or any orb to play a send.

5. Through the day (dynamic)

One orb whose scene follows the clock through ten frames: midnight moon, blue hour, dawn, morning meadow, seaside morning, summer sky, golden afternoon, harbor sunset, evening town, fireflies. Neighbouring scenes cross-fade over 40 minutes (less when a frame is short). Daytime frames are spaced by the length of the day. The place sets sunrise and sunset, so the dawn and sunset scenes land at the real times there ("Clock only" uses fixed 6:30 and 19:00). Drag the time, press Now, or Play a day to see 24 hours in 12 seconds.
